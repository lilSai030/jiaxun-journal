My graduate research explored gender inequality, emotional labour and intergenerational relationships through interviews and social media discourse analysis.

## Questions

How do private experiences of care, tension and family intimacy become public conversations online? What do these narratives reveal about gendered expectations?

## Approach

Qualitative interviews and analysis of online discourse, interpreted through a gender and social justice lens.
