Questions

I keep coming back to the ways people make sense of themselves, their relationships and the rules they inherit.

Research / Qualitative inquiry / Gender & culture
Graduate research2025—2026

Mother–daughter relationships on the Chinese internet

Graduate research on gender inequality, emotional labour and intergenerational relationships, drawing on qualitative interviews and online discourse analysis. A study of how intimate experiences become public conversation.

Methods: interviews · discourse analysis · qualitative interpretation

Undergraduate researchSelected study

Fandom, fiction & gender representation

Research on Chinese BL fiction and online fan cultures using qualitative content analysis and digital media research. It sits somewhere between my academic interests and the kinds of stories I love to read and write.

Methods: textual analysis · digital culture research

in practice.

Research is one way to listen. Organising, teaching and working alongside communities are others.

Community / Programmes / Collaboration

Behind the numbers.

Much of this work happened in spaces that should stay private. Rather than publish participant photos or conversations without permission, this archive focuses on what I did, the scale of the work and what it required.

Independent / Digital community2022—2025

A community of 700,000+ people

Managed LGBTQ+ social channels, developed research-based editorial content and coordinated virtual events with international speakers. The work demanded a balance of communication, trust, logistics and attention to what audiences needed.

Community programmesSelected organisations

Working where people are

At Butterfly, contributed to outreach, a book launch, panels and workshops around anti-discrimination, migrant rights and inclusion. At Cell Count, researched community resources, edited contributions and coordinated deliverables supporting advocacy and outreach.
