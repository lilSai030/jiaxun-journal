## Overview

Between 2022 and 2025, I managed LGBTQ+ social accounts reaching **700K+ followers**, developed research-based editorial content, and coordinated webinars with international speakers.

## Selected scope

- Produced three research-based articles per week.
- Coordinated online webinars for **400–1,000 attendees**.
- Reported **23% year-over-year sign-up growth** and a **30% engagement increase** through content strategy and analytics.
